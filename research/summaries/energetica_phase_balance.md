**1) What the paper does**  
Reports an office order by Rajasthan DISCOMs mandating phase balancing at the distribution transformer (DT) level for single-phase rooftop solar connections. It instructs field officers to allocate new solar systems to the least-loaded phase and perform periodic phase reshuffling without delaying connection releases.

**2) Data used + whether public/Indian**  
Not stated (news report covering Indian DISCOM policy directives).

**3) Method + key numbers/results**  
* **Date:** March 02, 2026.  
* **Target systems:** Single-phase rooftop solar connections, particularly systems "up to 5 kW", across Jodhpur Discom, Ajmer Discom, and Jaipur Vidyut Vitran Nigam Ltd. (JVVNL).  
* **Problems cited:** Unplanned solar concentration on the same electrical phase causing DT-level load imbalance, reverse power flow, voltage rise, inverter tripping, transformer overheating, and power quality deterioration.  
* **Rules:** Allocate new connections to the least-loaded phase, conduct periodic phase reshuffling where required, and ensure solar connection release is not delayed due to balancing works.  
* **Data needed by DISCOM to comply:** Phase-wise load and solar capacity data at the DT level to identify the least-loaded phase.
