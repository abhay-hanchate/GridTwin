**1) What the paper does**  
Presents a brief utility success case where Spanish DSO UFD and Adaion deployed an LV grid digital twin to monitor real-time voltage and congestion while calculating generation and consumption hosting capacity.

**2) Data used + whether public/Indian**  
Geographical Information System (GIS) data and measurements from smart meters and other field devices from UFD (Spain). Not public (proprietary utility data) and not Indian.

**3) Method + key numbers/results**  
* **Inputs:** GIS data, smart meter measurements, and other field device measurements.  
* **Method:** Integration of GIS topology and live meter/field telemetry into a grid digital twin (mathematical/algorithmic formulations not stated).  
* **Outputs:** Real-time visibility on voltage behavior, over- and undervoltage events, congestion, network imbalances, and hosting capacity for generation/consumption.  
* **Forecasting / Corrective actions:** Not stated.  
* **Key numbers/results:** Successfully deployed as a proof-of-concept across 52 LV networks of UFD.

**4) Code/repo link if stated**  
Not stated (commercial vendor Adaion linked, no code repository).

**5) Limits or red flags**  
This is a short promotional case-study webpage summary with zero mathematical equations, architectural specifics, data schemas, or open implementations.

**6) Verdict for GridTwin: useful / not useful and why**  
Not useful. While it confirms industry use of GIS + smart meter digital twins for LV solar hosting capacity, it provides no technical methodology, formulas, code, or datasets for GridTwin development.
