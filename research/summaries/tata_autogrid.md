1) **What the paper does**  
Announces a pilot partnership between Tata Power-DDL and AutoGrid deploying an AI-based behavioral demand response program in North Delhi to reduce peak loads. Outlines a roadmap for future expansion into automated demand response, storage, and solar PV integration.

2) **Data used + whether public/Indian**  
Smart meter data from 4,000 residential consumers in North Delhi (Indian). Not public (utility proprietary data).

3) **Method + key numbers/results**  
*Method:* AI-enabled flexibility platform (AutoGrid Flex) providing day-ahead peak-reduction notifications (email, SMS, calls) with participation-linked incentives; phased roadmap includes DERMS, automated HVAC/AC control, and Solar PV/storage integration.  
*Key numbers:* Phase 1 spans 3 months (1st July to 30th September 2021) across 4,000 residential smart meter consumers; utility serves 7 million populace; AT&C losses reported at 7.3% (down from 53% in July 2002).  
*Coverage:* Does not cover voltage. Solar PV is only cited as a prospective future phase for DER grid balancing.
