**1) What the paper does**  
Describes JVVNL's Digital Utility Enhancement and Transformation (DUET) initiative to deploy a predictive distribution digital twin across Jaipur. Outlines network asset digitization, real-time power flow simulation, stress/demand forecasting, and distributed solar integration.

**2) Data used + whether public/Indian**  
Indian utility operational data (JVVNL, Rajasthan; proprietary/not public), including SCADA inputs, smart/metering data, GIS asset surveys, field inspections, and consumer feedback.

**3) Method + key numbers/results**  
- **Method:** Digital twin modeling developed with the Global Energy Alliance for People and Planet and technology partner Edge Electra; integrates GIS, SCADA, and metering to simulate power flows, track asset health, and forecast network stress and demand in real time.  
- **Key numbers:** Covers a utility base of over 5.5 million consumers; >1.4 million assets (poles, lines, transformers, substations, service lines) geomapped/tagged toward a target of >4.5 million assets; integrates >2,000 MW commissioned PM-KUSUM solar (10,000 MW in pipeline); projected annual savings exceeding Rs 2 billion.

**4) Code/repo link if stated**  
Not stated.

**5) Limits or red flags**  
Industry executive article lacking technical formulations, load-flow algorithms, open datasets, and specific mathematical models for rooftop solar voltage regulation or hosting capacity calculation.

**6) Verdict for GridTwin: useful / not useful and why**  
**Useful.** Provides concrete institutional context, vendor details (Edge Electra / GEAPP), digitised asset scope (1.4M to 4.5M assets down to service lines), and operational use cases (power-flow simulation, stress/demand forecasting, PM-Surya Ghar and KUSUM integration) for JVVNL's DUET digital twin.
